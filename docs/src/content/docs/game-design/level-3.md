# Level 3 — Meltdown (Escape)

**What only this level has:** a chase, the thruster double-jump, lava, and a set-piece finale at the reactor core.

## Objective

Escape the collapsing corridor, then bring three stabiliser pylons online, cross the bridge they extend, and **hold E for 3 s** to seal the core before the countdown ends (3:20 on Normal).

## Sections

| Section | Challenge |
|---|---|
| A · Collapse | Debris falls from the broken ceiling. A red ring appears where each piece will land. Vault over fallen girders |
| B · Molten pit | The floor is gone. Jump across platforms, ride a moving platform, and make one big gap that needs the new **thruster double-jump** |
| C · Pistons | Crushing pistons slam down in a rolling sequence. Wait for one to rise, then dash under it |
| D · Reactor chamber | Three pylons on raised platforms (climb the stepping blocks). The core fires **plasma flares** at where you're heading. With all three online, the bridge extends to the sealing console |

## Mechanics

- **The fire wall:** a wall of fire advances behind you from the start.
  - Its speed adjusts to keep the chase tense but fair: faster when you're far ahead, slower when it's close.
  - If it catches you, SPARK reboots at the last checkpoint and the fire is pushed back.
  - A blast door shuts it out when you reach the chamber.
- **Thrusters:** press Space again in mid-air. Flames appear on SPARK's backpack.
- **Lava:** falling in costs 34 health and returns you to the checkpoint.
- **The reactor core:** a custom shader that boils harder as the timer runs down and calms as each pylon comes online (its containment rings lock into place). Sealing it cools it from orange to blue.
- **Heat:** a post-processing heat haze grows near the fire and the core.
