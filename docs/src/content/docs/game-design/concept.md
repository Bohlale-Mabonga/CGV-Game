# Concept

You are a small maintenance robot inside a space station reactor that is overheating. The station will melt down unless you reach and seal the reactor core.

The game is split into three levels, each built around a different kind of challenge rather than reusing the same mechanic with new scenery:

1. **Corridors (Explore).** Navigate dark corridors using a flashlight, avoid steam vents, collect keycards.
2. **Control Room (Solve).** Route power through junctions in the correct order before a countdown runs out, while avoiding a sweeping security beam.
3. **Meltdown (Escape).** Race through a collapsing corridor to reach and seal the glowing reactor core before a second countdown expires.

This premise was chosen because it gives a natural reason for dramatic lighting (dark corridors, glowing core), hierarchical objects (doors, junctions, reactor parts), and one clear custom shader (the reactor core), without requiring AI-driven enemies, a full physics engine, or complex flight controls.
