# Concept

You are SPARK, a small maintenance robot inside a space-station reactor that is overheating. The crew have evacuated. The station will melt down unless you reach the reactor core and seal it.

The station AI, **ARIA**, guides you. She speaks with the browser's text-to-speech voice and through subtitles. Data logs left by the crew fill in the story and hide clues.

The game has three levels, each built around a different kind of challenge rather than reusing one mechanic with new scenery:

1. **Corridors (Explore).** The power is out. Explore dark corridors with a flashlight, time your way past steam vents, and recover three keycards.
2. **Control Room (Solve).** Restore power through five junctions, lowest load first, before the countdown runs out. Avoid a sweeping security sentry, then route the power at the master console.
3. **Meltdown (Escape).** Outrun a collapsing corridor and a wall of fire, cross a lava pit with thrusters, then stabilise and seal the reactor core.

The premise gives natural reasons for:
- **dramatic lighting:** dark corridors, a glowing core
- **hierarchical objects:** the robot, doors, the turret, junctions, the reactor's containment rings
- **a centrepiece custom shader:** the reactor core

Each level also brings its own visual identity:
- Level 1 is dark and cold, with red emergency lights.
- Level 2 is bright, under a glass dome with an animated nebula sky.
- Level 3 is red-orange, with heat haze and lava.
