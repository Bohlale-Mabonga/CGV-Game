# Gameplay

## Objectives and progress

Each level shows its current objective in the HUD, and the objective updates as you progress (for example *Find the reactor keycards (2/3)*). Completing a level shows a results screen with:
- time and best time
- reboots, damage taken, hints used
- data logs found

## HUD

- Level name and objective (top left)
- Countdown timer (Levels 2 and 3), which pulses red in the last 30 seconds and beeps for the last 10
- Integrity (health), stamina and flashlight battery bars
- Collected keycards, the scanner cooldown, and unlocked abilities
- Minimap in the top-right corner
- Interaction prompt, with a ring that fills while you hold E
- Sentry detection meter (Level 2)
- ARIA subtitles, hints and pop-up notifications

## Failure, checkpoints and loss

- **Integrity (health):** SPARK has 100. Hazards take damage and knock you back:
  - steam vents: 22
  - the sentry zap: 25
  - debris: 30
  - pistons: 40
  - lava: 34
- **Reboot:** at 0 integrity, SPARK shows a "system failure" glitch effect and reboots at the last checkpoint with full integrity. Checkpoints are set automatically as you progress.
- **Losing a level:** in Levels 2 and 3 you lose when the countdown hits zero. You can retry the level or restart the game, with no page refresh.

## Winning

Sealing the reactor core at the end of Level 3 turns the core from molten orange to calm blue. The camera then orbits the core, and the victory screen shows totals for the whole run and a **rank (S–D)**. The rank is based on:
- time
- reboots
- hints used
- mistakes
- data logs found
- difficulty

## Replay value

- **Randomised puzzles:** the Level 1 door code and the Level 2 junction loads, units and routing grid are new every run.
- **Records:** best times per level, best full run and best rank are saved.
- **Difficulty:**

  | Difficulty | Timers | Damage | Sentry and fire wall |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Story | ×1.5 | ×0.5 | slower |
  | Normal | standard | standard | standard |
  | Hard | ×0.8 | ×1.5 | faster |

- **Data logs:** 7 to find across the three levels.

## Help

- **H:** gives progressively more detailed hints, up to the full answer.
- **Stuck prompt:** after 75 s without progress, the game suggests pressing H.
- **Q:** the scanner reveals items through walls.
